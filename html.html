ROBOTICS

Robotics is the interdisciplinary study and practice of designing, building, operating, and using machines (robots) capable of performing physical tasks. It combines mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, computer science, and artificial intelligence.

  • Mechanical structure & actuators: The physical body< /li>
  • Sensors: Components that gather data from the surroundings or the robot itself
  • Control system & software: Processors, algorithms, and AI models
  • Power source: Batteries, electrical grids, solar panels
